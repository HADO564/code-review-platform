# Letter of Resignation

**Date:** August 4, 2026

Dear Shahid Zadran,

Please accept this letter as formal notification of my resignation from my position at Frontair, effective 18th August, or sooner if you wish.

This was not an easy decision, but after careful consideration I believe it is the right one. A few factors have led me to this point:

- **Workload:** The scope and pace of work have become excessive and unsustainable. I have consistently taken on more than a healthy workload allows, and it has come at the cost of my well-being. I signed up as a backend developer, then I worked with the designs of the frontend, then I was made to work on the electron app, and then made to hire not just backend but also frontend interns. This was not what I signed up for, to say the least.
- **Lack of recognition:** Despite the level of effort and hours invested, this work has not felt rewarding — the contributions I've made have gone largely unacknowledged, both professionally and in terms of growth opportunities. Initially working with AWS and developing an AI tool felt like a new experience, but considering the current vision, all the subsequent product ideas strike me as GPT wrappers, which is not the direction I want my professional life to diverge towards.
- **Compensation:** My current pay does not reflect the responsibilities I carry or the market value of the role, and repeated attempts to address this have not led to meaningful change.
- **Misalignment on vision:** Over time, it's become clear that my perspective on the direction of the company and product no longer aligns with yours. This divergence makes it difficult for me to continue contributing effectively or with full conviction.
- **Career direction:** I've also come to realize that my long-term interests lie outside backend development. I'm looking to transition into embedded systems and hardware engineering, and pursuing that path requires a level of focus and reskilling that isn't compatible with continuing in my current role.

I'm grateful for the opportunities I've had here and for the people I've worked alongside. I'll do everything I can to ensure a smooth transition over the coming weeks, including documenting my work and helping identify or train a replacement.

Thank you for your understanding.

Sincerely,
Haider Muhammad Wazir
